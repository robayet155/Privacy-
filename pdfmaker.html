PDF Maker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

PDF Maker ("the app") lets you scan documents and convert images to PDF files and PDF files to images. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts and does not require an internet connection to work.

Your files and photos

Images you pick or capture, and the PDFs you create, are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. PDFs you create are stored in the app's private storage on your phone until you delete them. You can share or save them yourself using the options inside the app.

Camera and files access

When you choose to scan, the app opens your device's camera app to take a picture. When you choose to add images or PDFs, the app opens the system file picker. The app only receives the files you select.

Third-party services

The current version does not use third-party analytics or advertising services. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
